# antier-blog-image-generator

Generates Antier blog banner images in the style of https://www.antier.com/blogs/.

**Before designing or generating any banner, read `BRAND.md`. It is mandatory and overrides everything else.**

Key rules (details in BRAND.md):
- Canvas 931×551 (export @2x). The Antier mark (`brand/logo/antier-mark-on-dark.svg`) sits top-left: 4.3% of the width, 34 px margin.
- Headline: the brief's `BANNER-` line only. Almarai Regular, white, one size, ~30 px at 931 wide, 2 lines, left-aligned with the logo, vertically centred.
- Imagery: dark, cinematic 3D stock (Magnific / Freepik) with one glowing blue/cyan hero object on the right. The left ~45% stays dark for text.
- Don't use images from the Antier Figma file, ring-of-icons diagrams, text panels, highlighted words, or extra text.
- Visual references live in `references/`.
